# 📊 Blinkit Sales Performance Analysis | Power BI

## Overview

Blinkit Sales Performance Analysis is an interactive Business Intelligence project developed in Power BI to analyze sales performance, product categories, outlet operations, and customer behavior. The dashboard transforms raw sales data into actionable insights, helping stakeholders make data-driven decisions through dynamic visualizations and KPI monitoring.

## Project Objectives

* Analyze overall sales performance across Blinkit outlets.
* Identify top-performing product categories and outlet types.
* Evaluate outlet performance based on size, location, and establishment year.
* Understand customer preferences through ratings and product visibility.
* Generate business recommendations to improve sales and operational efficiency.

## Dashboard Pages

### 1. Executive Overview

Provides a high-level summary of business performance through key metrics such as:

* Total Revenue
* Average Sales
* Total Products Sold
* Average Customer Rating
* Total Outlets

### 2. Product Analysis

Analyzes product categories to identify:

* Best-selling product categories
* Revenue contribution by fat content
* Customer ratings across categories
* Product category performance comparison

### 3. Outlet Analysis

Evaluates outlet performance by:

* Outlet Type
* Revenue Generation
* Average Sales
* Customer Satisfaction Ratings

### 4. Location Analysis

Compares sales performance across different outlet location tiers:

* Tier 1
* Tier 2
* Tier 3

and identifies high-performing geographical segments.

### 5. Customer Insights & Recommendations

Provides advanced analytics through:

* Smart Narrative
* Q&A Visual
* Customer Rating Analysis
* Product Visibility vs Sales Relationship
* Business Recommendations

## Key Insights

* Tier 3 outlets contribute the highest share of sales.
* Medium-sized outlets generate strong revenue performance.
* Certain product categories significantly outperform others in sales.
* Product visibility influences sales performance.
* Customer ratings remain consistently high across most outlet types.

## Business Recommendations

* Expand operations in high-performing Tier 3 locations.
* Increase inventory for top-selling product categories.
* Improve visibility for low-performing products.
* Focus marketing efforts on highly rated product segments.
* Optimize outlet expansion strategies based on size and location performance.

## Conclusion

This project demonstrates how Power BI can be used to transform retail sales data into meaningful business insights. By leveraging DAX calculations, interactive visualizations, and advanced analytics features, the dashboard enables effective monitoring of Blinkit's operational and sales performance while supporting strategic decision-making.
